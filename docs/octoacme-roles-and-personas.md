# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality standards, test strategy, and acceptance validation. They collaborate with developers, product managers, and project managers to ensure releases meet quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain end-to-end testing strategy (unit, integration, end-to-end, and performance)
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with features and acceptance criteria
- Lead QA execution and coordinate test environments and test data readiness
- Identify quality risks early and escalate blockers that impact release confidence
- Validate releases against acceptance criteria before deployment handoff

### Goals
- Ensure high quality and reliability in production
- Reduce defects, regressions, and escaped bugs
- Enable fast, confident deployments through strong quality gates

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria reviews with product and development
- Quality checkpoints, defect triage, and release readiness reviews
- Incident response and post-release quality retrospectives

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design scalable, maintainable, and secure solutions while guiding technical decisions across teams. They partner with developers and product managers to evaluate trade-offs and align implementation with long-term strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate technical approaches and propose solution designs for complex initiatives
- Review architecture and designs for scalability, performance, reliability, and maintainability
- Identify technical risks and define mitigation plans with engineering teams
- Guide engineering standards, platform patterns, and technology choices
- Mentor developers and promote technical knowledge sharing across teams

### Goals
- Align technical decisions with business and product strategy
- Reduce technical debt and rework over time
- Maintain scalable, resilient architecture as the product grows

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design reviews with engineering contributors
- Technical risk assessments with project and product leadership
- Ongoing mentoring discussions and standards alignment sessions

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Officers ensure systems and processes meet security and regulatory requirements. They partner with product, engineering, and project teams to reduce risk while maintaining delivery velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Review planned and implemented features for vulnerabilities and compliance gaps
- Define security requirements, controls, and secure-by-default expectations
- Conduct security assessments and coordinate penetration testing activities
- Support incident response, investigation, and post-incident remediation planning
- Maintain compliance documentation and audit readiness artifacts

### Goals
- Protect systems, data, and customer information from threats
- Meet regulatory and contractual compliance obligations
- Build customer trust through consistent security practices

### Typical Communication
- Security and threat-modeling reviews during planning and design
- Compliance checkpoints with project managers and stakeholders
- Incident response coordination and audit preparation sessions

---

## DevOps & Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps & Infrastructure Engineers manage infrastructure and deployment pipelines to ensure reliable, observable, and efficient delivery. They collaborate with developers, project managers, and architects to operate stable production systems.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage production environments, infrastructure provisioning, and scaling
- Implement monitoring, logging, and observability standards
- Support incident response and root-cause analysis for operational issues
- Optimize infrastructure performance, reliability, and cost efficiency

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, repeatable deployments
- Maintain high system uptime and service performance
- Provide clear visibility into production health and delivery risks

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment execution coordination
- Infrastructure and reliability reviews with engineering and architecture
- Incident response collaboration and operational follow-up reviews

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads ensure features are intuitive, usable, and aligned with brand and accessibility standards. They partner with product and engineering teams to translate user needs into effective experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing to validate assumptions
- Create designs, flows, and prototypes aligned with user and business needs
- Review implementation quality for design fidelity and usability outcomes
- Advocate for user experience in prioritization and technical trade-off decisions
- Guide accessibility and inclusive design practices across features

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive features that improve user satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce user friction across critical workflows
- Build consistent, accessible experiences across the product

### Typical Communication
- User research readouts and design reviews with product and engineering
- Usability testing feedback sessions and iteration planning
- Acceptance criteria validation to align design intent and implementation
