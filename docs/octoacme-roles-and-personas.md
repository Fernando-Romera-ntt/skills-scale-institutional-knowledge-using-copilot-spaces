# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads own quality standards, test strategy, and acceptance validation. They collaborate with developers, product managers, and project managers to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality gates.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain testing strategy (unit, integration, end-to-end, performance)
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with features and acceptance criteria
- Lead QA execution and manage test environments
- Identify quality risks and escalate blockers
- Validate releases against acceptance criteria before handoff to deployment

### Goals
- Ensure high quality and reliability in production
- Reduce defects and regressions
- Enable fast, confident deployments

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria reviews with Product Managers and Developers
- Quality checkpoints and defect triage with Developers
- Escalation and release readiness updates with Project Managers

### Interaction with Core Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test coverage, defect resolution, and regression prevention.
- **Product Managers**: Align acceptance criteria, quality expectations, and release readiness.
- **Project Managers**: Communicate quality risks, blockers, and testing timelines.

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design scalable, secure solutions and guide technical decisions. They partner with developers, product managers, and project managers to evaluate trade-offs and shape the technical roadmap.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate technical approaches and propose solutions for complex problems
- Review designs and architecture for scalability, performance, and maintainability
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Guide standards and technology choices
- Mentor developers and support knowledge sharing

### Goals
- Ensure technical decisions align with long-term strategy
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Maintain system scalability and performance

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions with Developers
- Technical trade-off conversations with Product Managers
- Risk assessments and delivery impact updates with Project Managers

### Interaction with Core Roles
- **Developers**: Provide architecture guidance, standards, and mentoring.
- **Product Managers**: Translate product direction into sustainable technical plans.
- **Project Managers**: Surface technical risks and planning implications early.

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure systems and processes meet security and regulatory standards. They partner with product, engineering, and operations to manage risk and compliance requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Review features and systems for security vulnerabilities and compliance gaps
- Define security requirements and controls
- Conduct security assessments and penetration testing
- Manage incident response and post-incident reviews
- Maintain compliance documentation and audit readiness

### Goals
- Protect systems and data from threats
- Meet regulatory and compliance obligations
- Build customer trust through security practices

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews with Developers during implementation and release preparation
- Compliance checkpoints with Product Managers for requirement alignment
- Incident response and audit coordination with Project Managers

### Interaction with Core Roles
- **Developers**: Integrate secure coding practices and remediate vulnerabilities.
- **Product Managers**: Ensure security and compliance requirements are reflected in scope.
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate timelines for audits, controls, and incident follow-up.

---

## DevOps & Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Infrastructure Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and operational excellence. They enable teams to ship code reliably and scale systems.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and CI/CD infrastructure
- Manage production environments and infrastructure scaling
- Implement monitoring, logging, and observability
- Support incident response and post-incident root cause analysis
- Optimize infrastructure costs and performance

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments
- Maintain system uptime and performance
- Provide visibility into production health

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment execution with Developers and Product Managers
- Infrastructure design reviews and reliability updates with Developers
- Incident response coordination and operational reporting with Project Managers

### Interaction with Core Roles
- **Developers**: Support build/release automation, environment consistency, and observability.
- **Product Managers**: Align release windows and operational constraints with product priorities.
- **Project Managers**: Communicate infrastructure risks, dependencies, and incident timelines.

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX and Design Leads ensure features are intuitive, usable, and aligned with brand standards. They collaborate with product and developers to validate user needs and design solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create designs and prototypes aligned with user needs and brand standards
- Review implementations for design fidelity and usability
- Advocate for user experience in trade-off discussions
- Support accessibility and inclusive design practices

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, usable features that delight users
- Reduce friction and support adoption
- Build consistent, accessible user experiences

### Typical Communication
- User research readouts and design reviews with Product Managers and Developers
- Usability testing feedback and design handoff sessions with Developers
- Scope and acceptance alignment for UX quality with Project Managers

### Interaction with Core Roles
- **Developers**: Partner on design implementation, usability, and accessibility fidelity.
- **Product Managers**: Align user outcomes, feature priorities, and validation criteria.
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate design milestones, dependencies, and stakeholder feedback.
